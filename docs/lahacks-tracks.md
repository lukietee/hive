# LA Hacks 2026 (AI Hackathon) — Tracks & Sponsor Challenges

Source: https://live.lahacks.com/tracks (via /api/content)

## Tracks

### TRACK 1: Agentverse (sponsored by Fetch.ai)

Search & Discovery of Agents: Build and Register AI Agents on Agentverse, discoverable via ASI:One, that turn user intent into real, executable outcomes.

### TRACK 2: Model Learning

Build systems that learn, adapt, and improve from data. Create innovative models that uncover patterns, make predictions, or solve complex problems.

### TRACK 3: Beyond the Digital

Build technology that brings intelligence into the physical world. This track challenges hackers to create projects using embedded AI, hardware, augmented reality, virtual reality, or other emerging interfaces to make devices and environments more adaptive, interactive, and immersive.

### TRACK 4: Applied Intelligence

Use AI to solve real-world problems beyond traditional AI applications. Build practical tools, products, or systems that apply intelligent technology to improve everyday experiences, industries, and communities. Projects may focus on automation, accessibility, education, healthcare, or any other meaningful application of AI.
