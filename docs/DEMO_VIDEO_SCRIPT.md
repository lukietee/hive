# Hive Demo Script (~1:30)

## 0:00 The widget

HIVE is a desktop widget that lives directly at the top of your screen. It has access to every single file and application on your computer.

*Show: hover the notch, it opens to "Ask Hive to…", then collapses.*

## 0:10 Text it from anywhere

You actually don't need to be at your computer to use HIVE. Just text Hive: "investors are here in five, get Whisk ready." Hive's Conductor reads the task and builds a team for it.

*Show: the text on your phone, a new group chat, agents joining ("➕ Fixer", "➕ Web"…).*

## 0:22 A swarm on one computer

Every agent gets its own cursor, so a whole team works your computer at the same time without touching your mouse. One fixes the bug in VS Code and gets the tests passing. Another finishes the website and deploys it live. One builds the pitch deck.

And they can do a lot more: order food on DoorDash, shop on Amazon, post to Instagram, even call a business for you.

*Show: the 2×2 screen, VS Code going to 5/5, the live Vercel site, slides filling in.*

## 0:45 It asks before it decides

For critical decisions, like spending money or conflicting information, Hive texts you the options and waits for your call. Here, the Checker found two different prices, so I just reply with what I want.

*Show: the Checker's question, your "1", the Instagram post.*

## 0:55 Examples

Here's HIVE rushing to fix the landing page, pitch deck, and social media post right before an investor meeting.

*Show: the Pitch in 5 run (VS Code, the live site, the deck, the Instagram post).*

And here's another example of HIVE organizing a birthday party by calling local bakeries for a cake, DoorDashing food, and sending out invites.

*Show: the bakery call and Call window, the DoorDash cart, invites in Messages.*

## 1:20 Always in control

And if you ever change your mind, just text "cancel" and every agent stops right where it is. So wherever you are, you've got a whole team working on your computer for you. That's HIVE.

*Show: you text "CANCEL", every window closes, the notch collapses.*
