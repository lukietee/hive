## Full voiceover (read-through)

> HIVE is a desktop widget that lives directly at the top of your screen. It looks like part of your Mac — until you give it a job.
>
> You don't need to be at your computer. Just text Hive like a friend: "investors are here in five, get Whisk ready." Hive's Conductor reads the task and builds a team for it.
>
> Every agent gets its own ghost cursor, so a whole team can work your computer at the same time — without touching your mouse. One fixes the bug in VS Code and gets the tests passing. Another finishes the website and deploys it live. One builds the pitch deck, slide by slide.
>
> When agents disagree, they ask you. The Checker caught two different prices — I just reply "1". Then Social designs a launch graphic and posts it to Instagram.
>
> Hive isn't just for work. "Plan Jason's birthday party, October 19th at 9, pizza, three friends." It finds a bakery that's open and actually calls them to order the cake. It writes the invites, puts it on the calendar, and designs the invitation. Food and decorations go in the cart — but nothing is bought without your approval.
>
> And if anything goes wrong, one word stops everything.
>
> Hive. Your team, on your computer, from anywhere.

*(about 230 words · about 1:30)*

## Recording tips

- **Record the two runs separately** and cut them together. The live runs take a few minutes each; speed up the middle (2–4×) and keep the key moments (the "1" reply, the call, the YES/NO, CANCEL) at normal speed.
- **Show the phone and the Mac in the same shot** for the texting moments (picture-in-picture, or iPhone Mirroring on the Mac).
- **Record the bakery call audio** on the phone or with the bakery's consent; a teammate can play the bakery if no bakery is available.
- **Don't show keys or `.env`** on screen.
